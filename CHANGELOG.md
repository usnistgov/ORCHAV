## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-01

- Includes the ORCHAV Generator, Visualizer, Shared Data Layer, and curated
  scenarios.
- Provides documentation for installation, scenario workflows, visualization,
  data handling, and licensing.
- Includes lightweight scene, target, texture, and neutral IBL assets required
