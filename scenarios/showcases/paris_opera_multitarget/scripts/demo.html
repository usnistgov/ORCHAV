Surfaces produce the echoes

Cars, scooter, walking body and drone are triangle meshes. Specular and diffuse returns come from ray tracing their surfaces.

Angle separates the scene

A 256-element planar receive array feeds a digital beam scan. Range, velocity and direction distinguish road traffic from the airborne target.

Tracks come from detections

CFAR, angular grouping and Cartesian tracking process the saved signals. Ground truth is used afterward to evaluate the reports.
